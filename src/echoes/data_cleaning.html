These features can reflect a poet’s mix of forms as well as their writing habits. They omit character identity, syntax, rhyme, and themes. A tight group in the chart is a starting point for comparing verse structure and reading the poems.

Character use

The Character use view counts individual Han characters from the same sampled poems. Each count is divided by the poet’s total sampled Han characters and expressed per 1,000. The shared vocabulary keeps characters found in at least 20% of sampled poets, rounded up, with a minimum of two poets.

PCA centers the rates for each character across poets. It preserves their original scale so that differences of the same size in usage rate count equally before projection. Loadings show which characters pull scores up or down on each selected axis.

Shared characters can reflect diction, imagery, or subject matter. This view counts single characters and ignores word boundaries and character order. Its PCs are fitted separately from the verse-structure PCs.
